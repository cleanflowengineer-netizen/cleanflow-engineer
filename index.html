ABOUT CLEANFLOW

Chemical Engineering for Better Water Management

Water is an important resource in manufacturing, processing, cleaning, cooling, and other industrial operations.

Poor water quality can contribute to equipment corrosion, scaling in pipelines and boilers, decreased production efficiency, higher maintenance costs, and environmental concerns.

CleanFlow Engineering addresses these challenges by combining water quality testing with customized water treatment chemical solutions.

🌎

OUR VISION

Our goal is to help industries make better water treatment decisions through laboratory-based analysis and chemical engineering expertise.
